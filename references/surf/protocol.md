# Surf Protocol — In-Week / Offseason at Home

CrossFit 3x/week at Top Training Club is the strength and metcon backbone.
This protocol fills the gaps CrossFit doesn't cover: paddle-specific
endurance, t-spine/shoulder mobility, the pop-up motor pattern, breath,
and a Zone 2 base (VO2 max 30 = highest-leverage target).

Layered, not all-or-nothing. Daily minimums are non-negotiable; the week
flexes around surf trips.

## Daily Minimum (10–15 min)

1. **T-spine trio** — cat-cow x10 slow, thread-the-needle 30s/side,
   prone chest lifts x10 (chin tucked, upper back does the work)
2. **10 perfect pop-ups** — squat-to-rotation, feet land at 45°, knees
   never touch. Stop when quality drops. This is motor wiring, not cardio.
3. **Mobility session** — the 16-min sequence from
   [posture-reset](../mobility/posture-reset.md)

Weekly check: BOLT score, morning, before coffee. Log it.

## Saturday Surf Circuit (30–40 min)

3–4 rounds, minimal rest between movements, 60–90s between rounds:

- Banded pull-aparts x20 — open-chain, scap endurance
- Single-arm band rows x15/side — pull-through mimicry, anti-rotation
- Scap push-ups x10 — shoulder blade glide for the pop-up
- Prone cobra hold 30–45s — paddle posture
- Pop-ups x10 — under fatigue, where the wiring pays off

Then intervals: 6–8 x 30s hard / 2 min easy (rower, bike, run, or swim).
Mirrors the paddle-out → sprint-for-wave → paddle-back-out pattern.

## Breath Work

- **BOLT score**: get a baseline, target 25s+, stretch goal 40s.
  Lives in prs.yaml under `breathwork`.
- **CO2 table (dry, 1–2x/week)**: ~8 holds at ~50% of max with decreasing
  rest. Seated or lying. Never in water, never alone in water, no
  hyperventilation.
- **Max hold test**: monthly, dry, seated. Log it.

## Cold Plunge (optional)

- Rest days or ≥4–6h after lifting — never right after CrossFit.
- 1–3 min, uncomfortable-but-calm. Use breathwork to stay relaxed; it's
  hold-down composure training as much as recovery.

## Pre-Trip Ramp (2–4 weeks before a surf trip)

- Swim shifts to intervals: 8x50m on 1:00, or 4x100m steady
- Pop-ups: 20/day, some inside the Saturday circuit (under fatigue)
- Saturday circuit: +1 round, rest drops to 60s
- Everything else stays the same — consistency beats cramming

## Benchmarks (log in prs.yaml, test monthly)

| Test | Metric |
|---|---|
| BOLT score | seconds, morning baseline (`breathwork` in prs.yaml) |
