# Surf References

Training knowledge for surf preparedness: paddle endurance, pop-up mechanics,
breath, mobility.

The goal: arrive at the water with enough physical headroom that attention
goes to wave reading and flow, not survival. Everest-level respect for the
ocean, flow-level intent. See [protocol.md](protocol.md) for the actual plan.

## What Surfing Actually Demands

| Demand | Reality | Train it with |
|---|---|---|
| Paddling | ~50% of session time; intermittent — long easy efforts + max bursts to catch waves | Swimming (best cross-training), open+closed-chain pulling, intervals |
| Paddle posture | T-spine extension + scapular stability held for hours; desk "turtle posture" is the #1 limiter | Daily t-spine mobility, prone chest lifts, erector endurance |
| Pop-up | Explosive motor skill; must be automatic before you're under stress | Land reps, quality over quantity, squat-to-rotation, feet land 45° |
| Hold-downs / duck dives | CO2 tolerance is the limiter, not lung capacity; the urge to breathe = rising CO2 | BOLT score, dry CO2 tables, calm under stress |
| Riding | Deep compressed stance, rotated hips, ankle dorsiflexion | Hip/ankle mobility, split squats, deep squat practice (CrossFit covers strength) |

## Core Sources

### Waterman 2.0 — Kelly Starrett & Phil White
The movement manifesto for surfers and paddlers, from the same lens as
[Supple Leopard](../mobility/supple-leopard.md). Movement baselines for
sustainable performance on and off the water; Standard #1: achieve and
maintain a stable spine. Identify and fix weakest links *before* adding
volume.

### Pre-Trip Conditioning Protocol — Boston Surf Adventures (Lineup Logic)
4-week ramp before surf travel. Key principles:
- Mobility before strength — adding strength to a restricted joint is a
  recipe for injury. Unlock the thoracic spine first.
- Daily 10-min t-spine flow: cat-cow x10, thread-the-needle 30s/side,
  prone chest lifts.
- Paddle work capacity = open-chain (band pull-aparts, single-arm band
  rows) + closed-chain (pull-ups, scap push-ups), high rep, short rest.
- Pop-ups: 10 perfect reps daily. Bad reps wire bad patterns — never train
  sloppy. Squat-to-rotation, not a burpee. Feet land at 45°.
- Conditioning: 30s hard / 2 min easy intervals mimic the paddle-out →
  sprint-for-wave cycle.

### Breath-Hold Training — Rapture Camps guide + freediving consensus
- Untrained adults hold 30–45s; trainable to 60–90s within weeks, well
  beyond typical hold-down durations.
- CO2 tolerance, not lung capacity, is the primary limiter — same thesis as
  [Oxygen Advantage](../breathwork/README.md). BOLT score is the baseline
  metric.
- Safety rules are absolute: never train breath holds in water alone, never
  hyperventilate before holds. Shallow water blackout kills experienced
  watermen. Dry land, seated or lying, only.

### Paddle Endurance — Rapture Camps / Surf Strength Coach
- Swimming is the single best cross-training for paddle endurance — even
  20 min 2x/week makes a noticeable difference.
- Most endurance loss in the water is technique, not fitness — but land
  prep builds the gas tank that technique stands on.
- Shoulder health is the long game: pull-aparts, scap stability, rotation
  work.

### Cold Exposure Timing — Roberts 2015 (J. Physiology) + consensus
- Cold immersion within ~4h of strength training blunts the hypertrophy and
  strength adaptation. Wait 4–6h, use rest days, or plunge in the morning
  before evening training.
- Timed right, it's useful for recovery, Panama heat management, and
  calm-under-stress training (pairs with breathwork).
