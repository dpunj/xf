# Strength Shake — Daily Reliability Anchor

Purpose: make nutrition dependable when home food is unreliable. This is not an optimized bodybuilding recipe; it is the easiest way to get protein, carbs, fiber, and micronutrients into the day without cooking.

Based on `~/notes/resources/nutrition.md` and current xf profile: 150 lb, lactose intolerant, CrossFit rebuild, Panama heat.

## Which Oats?

Best for shakes:

1. **Rolled oats / old-fashioned oats** — best default
2. **Quick oats** — acceptable; blend smoother
3. **Instant plain oats** — okay if unsweetened

Avoid:

- flavored instant packets
- granola
- oat cookies
- raw steel-cut oats — gritty unless soaked/cooked

Panama label search terms:

- **Avena en hojuelas**
- **Hojuelas de avena**
- **Avena integral en hojuelas**
- **Avena entera**

Start with **1/4 cup oats** for digestion. Move to **1/2 cup** if you want more calories/fullness and your stomach is fine.

## Base Recipe

Blend:

- 1 serving Orgain/Vega plant protein
- 1 banana, frozen if possible
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 handful baby spinach
- cinnamon
- 1-1.5 cups oat milk
- ice
- optional: 3 g creatine monohydrate

Adjust texture:

- Too thick → add oat milk or water
- Too chalky → add cacao or berries
- Not filling enough → use 1/2 cup oats or 2 tbsp PB
- Too heavy → use 1/2 banana or less PB

## Timing

Default: **noon / first real meal anchor**.

Also valid:

- morning if breakfast is unreliable
- post-CrossFit if dinner will be delayed
- emergency meal when the day is going sideways

The anabolic window is not the point. **Total daily protein is the point.**

## Weekly Protocol

For 7 days:

1. Make one shake daily.
2. Do not optimize mid-week.
3. Track only: made it? stomach okay? energy better? cravings lower?
4. After 7 days, adjust oats/PB/protein dose.

## Success Criteria

- Fewer chaotic food days
- 120g+ protein becomes easier
- Better CrossFit recovery
- Less decision fatigue around food
- Greens happen without moral drama

## Notes

Oat milk is great for taste but low in protein. If daily protein is still low, solve it by adding another protein hit later — eggs, meat, lentils/beans, or a second small shake — not by ruining the good shake.
