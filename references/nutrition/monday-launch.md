# Monday Nutrition Launch Plan

Goal: start early CrossFit with a reliable nutrition scaffold. Keep it simple for the first 7 days: one shake, hydration, creatine if available, and a solid dinner the night before.

## Shopping Checklist

### Required for the shake

- [ ] Plant protein powder — Orgain/Vega vanilla preferred
  - Panama picks: PriceSmart Orgain vanilla 920 g, Riba Smith Orgain Vanilla Bean, Orgánica Store Orgain Vanilla OG
- [ ] Oat milk
- [ ] Bananas
- [ ] Plain rolled oats
  - Look for **avena en hojuelas**, **hojuelas de avena**, **avena integral en hojuelas**, **old fashioned oats**
  - Avoid flavored packets, granola, oat cookies, raw steel-cut oats
- [ ] Peanut butter
- [ ] Baby spinach
- [ ] Ground cinnamon / **canela molida**
- [ ] Ice

### Hydration / Panama heat

- [ ] Limes
- [ ] Fine salt — **sal fina**, **sal marina fina**, or **sal yodada**
- [ ] Coconut water, optional
- [ ] Sparkling water, optional soda replacement

### Add-ons worth buying now

- [ ] Creatine monohydrate, unflavored powder
  - Best Amazon picks: NOW Sports Creatine Monohydrate or Optimum Nutrition Micronized Creatine
  - Size: **500 g first**; 1 kg only if price is much better and storage is dry
  - Local fallback: PriceSmart Nutrex Creatina Monohidratada 300 g, Vitamin Shoppe Creatine Powder, Riba Smith creatine, Orgánica Store Kiu Creatine
- [ ] Cacao powder — for Chocolate PB shake
- [ ] Frozen berries — for PBJ shake
- [ ] Frozen mango/pineapple — for tropical recovery shake
- [ ] Honey — for pre-WOD carb bottle if dinner was light

## Supplement Priority

1. **Protein powder / shake kit** — food reliability, not really a supplement
2. **Creatine monohydrate** — first true supplement; best strength/recovery ROI
3. **Magnesium glycinate** — add later if sleep/tension/recovery needs help
4. **Omega-3** — add later if not eating fatty fish 2x/week
5. **Zinc** — only if diet/labs suggest need; do not megadose

Do not add everything at once. The first win is consistency.

## Early CrossFit Morning

Assumption: wake **5:45-6:00am**, train **6:30-7:00am**.

### Wake

- AG1 with water
- 500-750 ml water + lime + small pinch fine salt
- Bulletproof coffee if desired/tolerated
  - If stomach feels heavy in class, use a smaller coffee or half the ghee/MCT
- Optional if dinner was light: 1 tsp honey in water or 1/2 banana

Do **not** force the full strength shake before class. It is too heavy for most early sessions. Bulletproof coffee is fat/caffeine, not protein or CrossFit fuel.

### During CrossFit

- Bring water
- Bring electrolyte limeade if hot/sweaty/long workout

### Post-WOD / mid-morning

Take the strength shake with creatine:

- 1 serving Orgain/Vega plant protein
- 1 banana, frozen if possible
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 handful baby spinach
- cinnamon
- 1-1.5 cups oat milk
- ice
- 3 g creatine monohydrate, if available

If appetite is low: use 1/2 banana but keep protein + creatine.

## Non-Training Day Default

Use the same shake as the first real meal/noon anchor. If food starts going sideways, make the shake immediately instead of waiting for a perfect meal.

## Night-Before Rule

Early training is won the night before.

Minimum dinner before 6:30/7am CrossFit:

- protein + carb + salt

Examples:

- chicken + rice
- dal + rice
- beef + rice
- eggs + toast
- soup + extra protein
- lentils/porotos + rice + extra protein if needed

## Electrolyte Limeade

Use on waking, pre-WOD, or during hot days.

- 750 ml water
- juice of 1 lime
- small pinch fine salt
- optional: splash coconut water
- optional: 1 tsp honey if underfed

It should taste lightly mineral/salty, not like seawater.

### If salt is rocky/coarse

Make salt concentrate:

- 1 cup water
- 1 tbsp coarse sea salt

Shake until dissolved. Add a small splash to lime water.

## 7-Day Rules

1. One shake/day minimum.
2. On early CrossFit days, shake goes **after** class.
3. Start oats at **1/4 cup**.
4. Creatine: **3 g/day** in the shake. If scoop is 5 g, half-scoop-ish or full 5 g are both fine.
5. Do not optimize mid-week.
6. Track only: made it, stomach okay, energy better, cravings lower.

## After 7 Days

Adjust one lever:

- Still hungry → 1/2 cup oats or 2 tbsp peanut butter
- Too heavy → 1/2 banana or less peanut butter
- Chalky → cacao powder or berries
- Protein still low → second small shake or bigger dinner protein
- CrossFit feels flat → better night-before carbs/salt, or 1/2 banana/honey pre-WOD
