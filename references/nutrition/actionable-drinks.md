# Actionable Drink Recipes

Goal: make Monday strong with low-friction drinks that cover protein, carbs, hydration, and micronutrients. All recipes are lactose-free and built around Divesh's current stack: plant protein, oat milk, banana, oats, peanut butter, spinach, cinnamon, creatine.

## Shopping List

### Core shake kit

- Orgain/Vega plant protein, vanilla preferred
- Oat milk
- Bananas
- Plain rolled oats / **avena en hojuelas**
- Peanut butter
- Baby spinach
- Ground cinnamon / **canela molida**
- Ice

### High-ROI add-ons

- Creatine monohydrate
- Cacao powder
- Frozen strawberries / mixed berries
- Frozen mango or pineapple
- Fine salt / **sal fina** or **sal marina fina**
- Limes
- Coconut water
- Sparkling water
- Ginger or mint
- Honey

## Daily Anchor: PB Banana Cinnamon Strength Shake

Use this as the default. Make it boringly repeatable.

**When:** noon / first real meal, post-CrossFit, or emergency meal.

**Blend:**

- 1 serving Orgain/Vega plant protein
- 1 banana, frozen if possible
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 handful baby spinach
- cinnamon
- 1-1.5 cups oat milk
- ice
- optional: 3 g creatine monohydrate

**Adjust:**

- Too thick → add water/oat milk
- Too chalky → add cacao or berries
- Not filling → 1/2 cup oats or 2 tbsp PB
- Too heavy → 1/2 banana or less PB

## Chocolate PB Strength Shake

Best-tasting variant. Use if plant protein tastes chalky.

**Blend:**

- Base strength shake
- + 1 tbsp cacao powder
- optional: extra ice

**Taste:** chocolate PB milkshake, but useful.

## PBJ Strength Shake

Best for fruit/fiber and making spinach disappear.

**Blend:**

- 1 serving plant protein
- 1 banana
- 1/4 cup rolled oats
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- handful baby spinach
- handful frozen strawberries or mixed berries
- 1-1.5 cups oat milk
- ice
- optional: 3 g creatine

## Colombian-ish Coffee Shake

Use when you want the coffee ritual plus actual nutrition.

**Blend:**

- 1 serving vanilla plant protein
- 1 banana
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1/4 cup oats
- cinnamon
- 1/2 cup cold coffee / cold brew
- 1 cup oat milk
- ice
- optional: 3 g creatine

Do not put creatine into hot coffee. Cold/room-temp is fine.

## Tropical Panama Recovery Shake

Good after sweaty CrossFit or hot walking days.

**Blend:**

- 1 serving vanilla plant protein
- frozen mango or pineapple
- 1/2 banana
- handful spinach
- 1/2 cup coconut water
- 1 cup oat milk or water
- squeeze of lime
- tiny pinch fine salt
- ice
- optional: 3 g creatine

## Pre-WOD Carb Bottle

Use 30-60 minutes before CrossFit if food has been light.

**Shake in bottle:**

- 500-750 ml water
- juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp honey or sugar
- small pinch fine salt

This is not a meal. It is fuel so class does not feel like death.

## Panama Electrolyte Limeade

Use daily in heat, before CrossFit, or when energy mysteriously sucks.

**Mix:**

- 750 ml water
- juice of 1 lime
- small pinch fine salt
- optional: splash coconut water
- optional: 1 tsp honey

It should taste lightly mineral/salty, not like seawater.

## Salt Concentrate for Rocky Sea Salt

Use if the salt is too coarse to pinch/dissolve.

**Jar:**

- 1 cup water
- 1 tbsp coarse sea salt

Shake until dissolved. Use a small splash in water/lime drinks.

## Lime Salt Soda

Healthy soda replacement.

**Build over ice:**

- sparkling water
- squeeze of lime
- tiny pinch fine salt or splash salt concentrate
- optional: mint

## Ginger-Lime Fizz

Use when you want something sharper.

**Build over ice:**

- sparkling water
- lime
- grated ginger or ginger juice
- tiny honey
- tiny pinch salt

## Hibiscus Soda

Good evening soda replacement.

**Prep:** make strong hibiscus tea, chill it.

**Build:**

- 1/2 glass chilled hibiscus tea
- 1/2 glass sparkling water
- lime
- optional honey

## Early CrossFit Schedule

Assumption: wake **5:45-6:00am**, train **6:30-7:00am**.

### If training at 6:30am

**5:45-6:00am — wake**

Keep the normal AM ritual, but do not let it replace hydration or post-WOD protein.

- AG1 with water
- 500-750 ml water + lime + small pinch fine salt
- bulletproof coffee if desired/tolerated
  - if stomach feels heavy in class, use a smaller coffee or half the ghee/MCT
- optional: 1 tsp honey or 1/2 banana if dinner was light

**Do not force the full strength shake before class.** Too heavy for most early sessions. Also: bulletproof coffee is fat/caffeine, not protein or CrossFit fuel.

**6:30am — CrossFit**

- Bring water.
- If the workout is long/sweaty, bring electrolyte limeade.

**7:45-8:30am — post-WOD anchor**

- Strength shake with 3 g creatine.
- If appetite is low, use the lighter version: 1/2 banana, 1/4 cup oats, 1 tbsp PB.
- If bulletproof coffee kills appetite, still get the shake in by mid-morning.

### If training closer to 7:00am

Same plan, but if hungry on waking:

- 1/2 banana + electrolyte limeade, or
- pre-WOD carb bottle

Then full shake after training.

### Night-before rule

Early training is won the night before. If dinner is light, morning performance suffers.

Minimum night-before dinner:

- protein + carb + salt
- examples: chicken/rice, eggs/toast, dal/rice, beef/rice, soup + extra protein

## 7-Day Launch Plan

Starting Monday:

| Day | Required | Optional |
|-----|----------|----------|
| Daily | 1 strength shake | 3 g creatine in shake |
| CrossFit days | AM electrolyte on waking + post-WOD strength shake | 1/2 banana or honey pre-WOD if dinner was light |

Rules:

1. One shake/day minimum.
2. On early CrossFit days, take the shake **after** class, not before.
3. Start oats at 1/4 cup.
4. Do not optimize mid-week.
5. Track: made it, stomach okay, energy better, cravings lower.
